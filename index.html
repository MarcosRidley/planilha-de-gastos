What comes in every month?

Your salary and anything else that arrives regularly. If something only lasts a while, set when it starts or ends. The 13th salary and bonuses come later, in One-offs.

What are your fixed bills?

Things that cost about the same every month: rent, condo fee, utilities, internet, insurance, subscriptions.

And the spending that varies?

A rough monthly estimate is fine. Your card statement from last month is a good place to look.

Paying anything in installments?

One row per purchase: the amount per installment, how many are left (counting the next one) and when the next one is due. They count down by themselves as months pass.

Anything coming up just once?

Money in or out in a specific month: 13th salary, a bonus, IPVA, IPTU, a trip, Christmas. Skip this if nothing comes to mind.

Do you have money saved?

Savings, investments, an emergency fund: anything you could use if you had to. Enter 0 if there's nothing yet.
